About the Project

The Digital Health Record Management System is designed to help maintain organized and accessible healthcare information for migrant workers in Kerala.

The Problem

Migrant workers may move between different locations for employment and may receive treatment from different healthcare facilities. Maintaining their medical information in an organized digital form can make it easier to access previous health records when required.

Project Objectives

Digital Records

Maintain organized digital health information for registered migrant workers.

Easy Access

Make it easier to search and access a worker's basic and medical information.

Continuity of Care

Help healthcare providers understand previous medical visits and treatments.

Better Organization

Reduce dependence on scattered or paper-based medical information.

Key Features

  • Migrant worker registration
  • Digital health record entry
  • Worker ID based health record search
  • Health information dashboard
  • Medical visit and treatment history
  • Simple and accessible user interface
SDG 3

Good Health and Well-being

Sustainable Development Goal 3 aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for people of all ages.

This project is aligned with SDG 3 by supporting better organization and accessibility of health information for migrant workers.

Project Scope

This front-end provides the user interface for the Digital Health Record Management System. The forms, search functionality and dashboard can be connected to a backend and database to create a complete healthcare record management application.
